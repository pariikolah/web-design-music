ABOUT

IVE (아이브) is a South Korean girl group formed by Starship Entertainment. The six members — Yujin, Gaeul, Rei, Wonyoung, Liz, and Leeseo — debuted on December 1, 2021 with the single "ELEVEN". Known for their confident, elegant image and catchy songs like "LOVE DIVE", "After LIKE", and "I AM", they quickly became one of the leading fourth-generation girl groups. Their fandom is called DIVE.

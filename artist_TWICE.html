ABOUT

TWICE (트와이스) is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment through the survival show SIXTEEN. The nine members — Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu — debuted on October 20, 2015 with "Like OOH-AHH". Famous for bright, catchy songs like "Cheer Up", "TT", and "What Is Love?", they are one of the most popular girl groups in K-pop, and their fandom is called ONCE.

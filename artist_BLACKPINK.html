ABOUT

BLACKPINK (블랙핑크) is a South Korean girl group formed in 2016 under YG Entertainment. The group consists of four members — Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa — and debuted on August 8, 2016 with "Square One". With hits like "DDU-DU DDU-DU", "Kill This Love", and "Pink Venom", they became one of the biggest girl groups in the world, and their fandom is called BLINK. Today all four members also run their own solo careers while continuing group activities together.

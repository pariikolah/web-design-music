ABOUT

IU (Lee Ji-eun / 아이유) is a South Korean singer-songwriter and actress who debuted in 2008 at just 15 years old. Known for her warm, emotional voice and her own songwriting, she has become one of the most beloved solo artists in Korea, with a long list of number-one hits. Alongside music she has built a successful acting career, and her fandom is known as UAENA.

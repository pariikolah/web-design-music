ABOUT

RED VELVET (레드벨벳) is a South Korean girl group formed by SM Entertainment. The group debuted on August 1, 2014 with the single "Happiness", and Yeri joined in 2015 to complete the five-member lineup — Irene, Seulgi, Wendy, Joy, and Yeri. Their name reflects two sides of their music: "Red" for bright, bold pop and "Velvet" for smooth, mature R&B. Their fandom is called ReVeluv.

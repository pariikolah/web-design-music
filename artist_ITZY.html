ABOUT

ITZY (있지) is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment. The five members — Yeji, Lia, Ryujin, Chaeryeong, and Yuna — debuted on February 12, 2019 with the single "DALLA DALLA". Known for their confident "girl crush" image, powerful choreography, and songs like "WANNABE" and "Not Shy", they became one of the leading fourth-generation girl groups. Their fandom is called MIDZY.

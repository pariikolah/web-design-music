ABOUT

LE SSERAFIM (르세라핌) is a South Korean girl group formed by Source Music, a label under HYBE. The name is an anagram of "I'M FEARLESS". The group debuted on May 2, 2022 with the mini album "FEARLESS" and now has five members — Sakura, Chaewon, Yunjin, Kazuha, and Eunchae. They are known for their strong performances and bold concepts, and their fandom is called FEARNOT.

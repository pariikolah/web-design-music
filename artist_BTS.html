ABOUT

BTS (Bangtan Sonyeondan / 방탄소년단) is a South Korean boy band formed in 2013 under HYBE (formerly Big Hit Entertainment). The group consists of seven members — RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook — who take part in writing and producing much of their own music. Known for socially conscious lyrics and genre-blending sound, BTS has become one of the best-selling and most influential music acts in the world, with a massive global fanbase known as ARMY.

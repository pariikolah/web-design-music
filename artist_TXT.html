ABOUT

TXT (TOMORROW X TOGETHER / 투모로우바이투게더) is a South Korean boy group formed by Big Hit Music under HYBE. The five members — Soobin, Yeonjun, Beomgyu, Taehyun, and Hueningkai — debuted on March 4, 2019 with the mini album "The Dream Chapter: STAR". They are known for telling stories about growing up through their music, and their fandom is called MOA.

ABOUT

NEWJEANS (뉴진스) is a South Korean girl group formed by ADOR, a label under HYBE. The group debuted in July 2022 with the single "Attention" and became famous for hits like "Hype Boy", "Ditto", "OMG", and "Super Shy". After a long legal dispute with ADOR, a Seoul court ruled in October 2025 that their contract stays valid, and the members returned to the agency. Danielle left the group after her contract was terminated, so NewJeans now continues with four members — Minji, Hanni, Haerin, and Hyein. Their fandom is called Bunnies.
